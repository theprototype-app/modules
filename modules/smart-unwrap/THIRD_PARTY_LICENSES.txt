Smart unwrap (xatlas) — third-party software
=============================================

This module ships, unmodified except as noted, a WebAssembly build of xatlas and the
JavaScript glue that drives it:

  assets/xatlas.wasm   the xatlas-wasm 0.1.3 binary, decoded byte-for-byte from the
                       package's inline (SINGLE_FILE) copy
  module.js            the xatlas-wasm 0.1.3 emscripten glue + wrapper, between the
                       "VENDORED xatlas-wasm glue" markers; two edits: the inline binary
                       is dropped (the .wasm above is loaded through
                       Module.instantiateWasm instead) and the ES `export {...}` block is
                       removed

Source package: https://www.npmjs.com/package/xatlas-wasm (version 0.1.3,
  sha512-LGWvSyHryDda9+a3+le2bd+QWPTDMFjfu3CPlVauTm8Liqd+S9wIjQScBzlOChFTyL8OpzZ4AI0tE0zoF3kc6g==)
Repository:     https://github.com/mode777/xatlas-wasm
